THE DETAILS

Frequently asked
questions.

What kinds of events does ROY manage?+

Weddings, Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, engagements, receptions, birthday parties, corporate events and other special occasions.

Can ROY manage décor and entertainment together?+

Yes. Our services include theme-based décor, artists, live music, DJs, anchors, emcees, choreography, welcome entries, effects and photobooths.

Do you provide catering and lighting?+

Yes. We support customised vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus, buffets, live counters, stage setup, professional lighting and ambience effects.

Does Roy Studios cover weddings?+

Yes. Roy Studios provides pre- and post-wedding shoots, cinematic wedding films and candid photography.
